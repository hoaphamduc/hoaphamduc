- 🔮 **Spiritual toolkit** — tarot, Vietnamese horoscope (tử vi), I Ching, numerology, daily card, and real astrology charts computed from exact birth time & place.
- 💌 **Letters to the future** — schedule a message to your future self.

### 🎁 [Quà Nhỏ — shopquanho.vn](https://shopquanho.vn) — *Personalized gift e-commerce* · `06/2026 – now`
3D night lights, photo keychains, music boxes, fridge magnets and more — customers design their gift right in the browser (upload photos, add names & dates) with a **live preview** before ordering. Includes combos & coupons, a gift-finder quiz, community photo wall, wishlist, COD / instant bank transfer, and nationwide shipping.

`Remix` `React` `Node.js` `Tailwind CSS` `MongoDB` `SePay` `Customeow` `Cloudflare`

### 🗺️ Hanoi Heritage Map — *FEF-R Project* · `03/2026 – now`
An interactive digital map of Hanoi's historical sites for the FEF-R project *"Heritage Tourism Journeys in Hanoi"*, funded by the Institut français du Vietnam, led by USSH-VNU with EFEO, Hanoi Architectural University and partners. Every temple, pagoda and communal house features history, opening hours, festivals, **360° photos, audio guides**, archival images and Hán-Nôm documents — organized into 4 themed journeys with turn-by-turn directions (car, motorbike, walking) in 🇻🇳 🇬🇧 🇫🇷.

`Node.js` `Express` `MongoDB` `Leaflet` `Leaflet Routing Machine` `Bootstrap` `Nginx`

### 🦻 Hear and Learn — *Learning platform for deaf & hard-of-hearing children* · `10/2024 – 12/2024`
A research project delivering interactive games and carefully crafted lessons — letters, vocabulary, sentences, and poems — to help children build listening and language skills.
- 🥇 1st Prize — Faculty of Special Education Scientific Research
- 🥈 2nd Prize — HNUE University-level Scientific Research
- 🥈 **2nd Prize — Euréka National Student Science Award**

`PHP` `MySQL` `HTML` `CSS` `JavaScript`

### 📜 Hán-Nôm Stele Rubbings Database — *Institute of Hán-Nôm Studies & EFEO* · `05/2025 – 06/2025`
A search system for **20,980 stele rubbings** made in Vietnam by the École française d'Extrême-Orient between 1910–1944, making them as easy to explore as possible.

`PHP` `HTML` `CSS` `JavaScript`
